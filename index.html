If emergency traffic starts, stop all other radio use. Bomb threat: no radio transmissions near a suspect item.

Licence conditions (226624)

  • Capacity 6,000. Monitor gate counters and report numbers to Ops.
  • Security ratio 1 per 500 patrons from opening.
  • Condition 9: “No more than 1 (one) open wine bottle and no more than 2 (two) open bottles of 375ml Soju or 4 (four) alcoholic beverage(s) can be sold or supplied to a person at any one time. From 9:00pm no more than 2 (two) alcoholic beverage(s) and 1 (one) open bottle of Soju 375ml can be sold or supplied to a person at any one time. Sale of wine bottles and bottles of 375ml Soju are to cease at 11:00pm. All other liquor sales are to cease 30 minutes prior to close.”
  • No alcohol brought in or taken out of the site.
  • Refuse entry or remove anyone wearing declared criminal organisation colours, jewellery or tattoos. Call SAPOL if safety is at risk.
  • Minors after 12:00am must be with a responsible adult.

Banned items

Weapons of any kind including knives, fireworks and flares, fire twirling gear, laser lights, drones, alcohol, illegal items, declared criminal organisation colours or emblems.

No routine bag checks. Check only if there's a concern about a suspicious package or alcohol (e.g. an esky). No metal detectors.

Code Pink - lost or found person

  • Radio "Code Pink Lost" or "Code Pink Found" with approximate age and description.
  • Stay with the person or the reporting guardian.
  • Found person: stay where they were found for 15 minutes before moving them.
  • Never escort a child alone. A security guard must always be present.
  • Offer water only, no food.
  • Check and record photo ID before handing over to a guardian.
